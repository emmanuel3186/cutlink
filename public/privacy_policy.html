cutlink Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At cutlink, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

1. Data We Collect from Account Creators

When you register an account or use Google Sign-In, we collect your verified email address, full name, and your platform account creation timestamp. This data is strictly used to securely authenticate your sessions and manage your personalized shortener dashboard.

2. Data We Process from Link Clickers

When an internet user clicks a cutlink URL, our servers temporarily process incoming non-identifying telemetry metrics. This includes the clicker's IP address (strictly used to approximate country and city location data), device operating system, browser type, and arrival timestamp. This information is instantly aggregated into analytical statistics for the link owner and does not reveal personal identities.

3. Data Sharing and Commercial Use

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share any personal identity logs or browser analytics to third-party data brokers or external marketing companies. Your data is used exclusively to keep your account operational.

4. Your Data Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to request access to your recorded personal data or request the permanent deletion of your profile account. To exercise your data rights, contact us at our designated legal portal.
